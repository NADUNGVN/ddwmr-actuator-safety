## B. Finding — the recommended scenario crosses the current theorem boundary

**Evidence.** MASTER §14 defines collision clearance from a **static circular** obstacle, with one fixed radius that includes the robot footprint. MASTER §15.2 defines a **one-hold** reachable tube under one voltage. Section 27 explicitly prohibits an unproved moving-obstacle claim. The CommonRoad scene has twelve **moving rectangles** with discrete states and a goal to be reached during a two-second window. Certifying this original task would require a proved continuous occupancy model and time-dependent collision set. With a hold shorter than two seconds it would also require selecting multiple voltages and reasoning over successive holds, which is beyond G2's one-hold result and approaches the unproved G3 policy/recursion problem. One could instead stipulate a two-second constant-voltage hold, or replace traffic by a conservative static circular enclosure, but each is a **new synthetic task/protocol choice**, not the unmodified CommonRoad benchmark. Its `10 m/s` road-vehicle initial speed has no sourced DDWMR/contact/voltage counterpart; this does not make the ODE undefined, but it supplies no practical robot relevance.

**Consequence.** Choosing the missing ego footprint, wheel/current state, and parameter values would still not close the scope mismatch. In particular, CommonRoad's `0.1 s` scenario sampling cannot silently become MASTER's voltage hold period. The proposal should not be advanced to a G2 manifest or called a source-backed DDWMR operating domain.

**Status:** **BLOCKED as a source-faithful G2 benchmark under unchanged MASTER.** A transparently source-inspired synthetic stress case remains possible, with distinct claims and separately reviewed assumptions; it does not establish practical usefulness or physical correspondence.
