## Required comparison standard

An actuator-unaware baseline can demonstrate model mismatch, but it cannot establish that a generic sampled-data safety theorem is inadequate when applied to the correct actuator model. At least one close generic method must receive the same actuator plant, voltage box, uncertainty information, measurements, and runtime budget as the proposed method, subject to the source method's actual assumptions. Report when an adaptation is needed and distinguish it from a faithful reproduction.

The research contribution must answer what a structured physical calculation adds: e.g. a constructive certified invariant region, tighter guaranteed hold enclosures at comparable cost, or an interpretable authority boundary whose assumptions and conservatism are characterized. None has been established by this initial screening.
