## Deliberately excluded generated material

Large or redundant generated material is not needed for the independent G3 literature/feasibility decision and is excluded from this public snapshot:

- temporary mutation workspaces and scratch directories;
- repeated full project source snapshots nested inside historical Auer runs;
- local Profil/BIAS build trees and object/binary outputs;
- downloaded/cached third-party source copies and old ZIP review bundles;
- unrelated legacy query batches and superseded preflight copies.

Exclusion is a publication-size and provenance decision. It does not delete or rewrite the local evidence; the working tree may retain those files. Do not infer a scientific result from an excluded artifact.
